What I Built

PoliceOSINT v1.0

AI-Powered Cyber Crime Investigation & Intelligence Platform · Gurugram Police

Next.js 14FastAPICelery RedisPostgreSQLDocker Groq/Llama-3.3-70bNginx

The Problem

Cyber fraud in India has led to losses of thousands of crores annually. Traditional investigation methods depend on victim complaints and manual data collection, leading to delayed detection and missed opportunities for preventive action.

The Solution

PoliceOSINT automates collection, analysis, and correlation of cyber threat intelligence from 50+ public sources including DuckDuckGo, Google News RSS, Reddit, Twitter/X, and Telegram channels. By combining OSINT collection with Groq/Llama-3.3-70b generative AI, the platform transforms raw digital intelligence into actionable, court-ready reports.

System Architecture

Data Ingestion Layer

  • DuckDuckGo HTML scraper — keyword-based scam search
  • Google News RSS — real-time scam campaign articles
  • Reddit API — r/scams, r/india, r/cybersecurity
  • Twitter/Nitter scraper — investment fraud tweets
  • Telegram monitor — real-time extraction via Telethon
  • Government feeds — CERT-In & Cybercrime.gov.in advisories

AI Analysis Pipeline

  • Stage 1: Heuristic regex engine — IOC extraction (UPI IDs, phone numbers, crypto wallets, URLs)
  • Stage 2: Groq/Llama-3.3-70b as digital forensic assistant
  • Drafts FIR notes, recommends IPC/IT Act sections
  • Explains threat patterns, guides investigation steps
  • Assigns preliminary risk scores (0–100)

10+ OSINT Investigation Modules

  • Identity Intel: Email breach, username enumeration, phone lookup
  • IP Intelligence: GeoIP, ASN, ISP, VirusTotal, TOR/VPN detection
  • Domain Analysis: WHOIS, DNS, SSL, subdomain enumeration
  • UPI Fraud: Risk scoring, fraud pattern detection, mule account indicators
  • Cryptocurrency: BTC/ETH/TRX tracing, mixer detection, cluster analysis
  • Threat Intel: MITRE ATT&CK mapping, IOC correlation
  • Dark Web: Ahmia search, credential leak monitoring
  • GEOINT: Reverse geocoding, IP-to-location mapping
  • Social Media: Cross-platform username search, profile risk scoring
  • Media Forensics: EXIF extraction, reverse image search, deepfake scoring
